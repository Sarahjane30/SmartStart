## Reviewing an onboarding plan
When an offer is accepted in iCIMS, NIA drafts an onboarding plan for HR to review. HR checks the start date, manager, mentor and laptop choice, then approves the plan. Nothing is sent to IT or the manager until HR approves.

## Document packet
The packet is complete only when every required form is uploaded and verified in iCIMS. A packet with any missing or rejected form stays Pending, and IT provisioning does not start.

## Document reminders
HR sends a document reminder when a packet is still incomplete 7 days before the start date, and again 3 days before. NIA drafts the reminder; HR reviews and sends it.

## Rework
If a form is rejected, HR marks the packet for rework with the reason so the joiner knows exactly what to fix. Rework resets the document stage to Pending.

## Closing a case
HR closes the case once the joiner is project ready. A closed case can be reopened if a problem appears in the first 30 days.
