## Standard new-hire kit
Every new hire receives the standard kit: a laptop, VPN, Microsoft 365, Jira and the engineering applications for their team. Extra software is requested separately through an access request so the standard kit is never delayed.

## Provisioning SLA
The IT provisioning target is 3 working days from the moment the ServiceNow request is created. A ticket that is still open after 3 days is marked as an SLA breach and appears in the IT queue and the Ops alerts.

## Laptop status
Hardware moves through Pending, Configured and Delivered on the ServiceNow ticket. Only Delivered counts as IT ready; Configured means the device is built but not yet with the joiner.

## When a ticket breaches
SmartStart sends the IT Service Desk a reminder when a ticket breaches SLA or when the start date is 3 days away and the laptop is not Delivered. Onboarding Operations may escalate a breached ticket to the IT lead. Nobody outside IT closes or edits a ServiceNow ticket.
