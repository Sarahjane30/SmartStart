## Before Day 1
Confirm the mentor, book Day-1 orientation and send a welcome note once IT setup is delivered. SmartStart reminds the manager when the start date is 5 days away and Day 1 or the mentor is still unconfirmed.

## Day-1 orientation
Orientation covers tools setup, security basics, team introductions and a first conversation with the mentor. Keep the first day light on deliverables and heavy on context.

## Choosing a mentor
Pick a mentor on the same team who has been in role for at least six months and can give the joiner an hour a week. The mentor is introduced on Day 1 and holds a weekly check-in for the first month.

## First week
Agree 30-day goals in the first week and schedule a weekly 1:1. Assign a small, well-scoped first project in Jira after orientation so the joiner can ship something early.

## First project
A good first project has a clear owner, three to five tasks, and does not depend on access the joiner does not yet have. Check project readiness before assigning it.
