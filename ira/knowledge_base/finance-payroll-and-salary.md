## When salary is paid
Salary is paid monthly and credited on the last working day of each month. If you join after the 20th, your first salary for the partial month is paid with the following month's payroll.

## Intern stipend
Intern stipends follow the same monthly cycle and are credited on the last working day of the month.

## Payslips
Payslips are published in the HR Portal under "Payslips" on the pay date. Payroll does not email payslips.

## Bank details
Add your bank account in the HR Portal before the 15th of your joining month so you are included in that month's payroll. Changes after the 15th apply from the next cycle.

## Tax
Declare your planned tax-saving investments in the HR Portal by the end of April each year. Form 16 is issued in the HR Portal by mid-June.

## Individual pay amounts
Your individual pay is confidential and shown only in your offer letter and payslips. IRA never displays or estimates individual pay.

## Questions
Raise payroll questions as an HR Portal case under "Payroll". Payroll aims to respond within 3 working days.
