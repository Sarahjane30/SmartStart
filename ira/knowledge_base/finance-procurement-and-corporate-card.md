## Buying for work
Goods and services for Waters must be bought from approved vendors through a purchase order (PO) in the procurement tool. Do not sign vendor contracts yourself; Procurement and Legal review them.

## Software and subscriptions
Never buy software or SaaS subscriptions with a personal or corporate card. Request software through the IT Service Desk so Information Security can review it first.

## Approval limits
Purchases up to 50,000 INR need manager approval. Purchases above 50,000 INR also need department head approval. Purchases above 5,00,000 INR need three competitive quotes.

## Corporate cards
Corporate cards are issued only to roles with regular travel, after manager request. Interns are not eligible. Card spend must be reconciled in the Expense tool within 30 days.

## New vendors
Request a new vendor through Procurement. Vendors must complete due diligence (tax, sanctions and security checks) before a PO can be raised.
