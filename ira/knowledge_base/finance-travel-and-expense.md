## What can be claimed
Reasonable business expenses approved by your manager can be claimed: travel, accommodation, meals while travelling, client meals approved in advance, and home-internet allowance where applicable.

## Submitting a claim
Submit expenses in the Expense tool (SAP Concur) within 30 days of the expense with an itemised receipt for anything above 500 INR. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Approval
Your manager approves claims. Finance Operations audits a sample and may ask for more detail. Reimbursement is paid with the next payroll after approval.

## Travel booking
Book flights and hotels through the approved corporate travel portal. Economy class applies to flights under 6 hours. Travel requires manager approval before booking.

## Daily meal limits
The domestic meal allowance while travelling is up to 1,500 INR per day. International limits are listed per country in the Expense tool.

## Not reimbursable
Alcohol (unless part of an approved client event), personal entertainment, traffic fines, and upgrades you choose are not reimbursable.
