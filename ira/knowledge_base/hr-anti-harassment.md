## Zero tolerance
Waters does not tolerate harassment, bullying or discrimination based on gender, religion, caste, age, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic. This applies at work, at work events, and in online channels such as Teams.

## Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)
Waters has an Internal Committee (IC) under the POSH Act. Complaints of sexual harassment can be raised in writing to the Internal Committee within 3 months of the incident. The IC contact list is published in the HR Portal.

## How to raise a concern
You can raise a concern with your manager, your HR Business Partner, the Internal Committee, or anonymously through the Ethics Line. You do not need to go to your manager first.

## Confidentiality and protection
Complaints are handled confidentially and only shared with those who need to know to investigate. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern or takes part in an investigation is itself a serious breach.

## Mandatory training
All employees complete POSH awareness training in the Learning Portal within 30 days of joining.
