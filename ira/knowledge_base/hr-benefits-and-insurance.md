## Health insurance
Full-time employees are covered by group medical insurance from their joining date. Cover extends to a spouse and up to two children. Enrol dependents in the HR Portal under "Benefits" within 30 days of joining.

## Interns
Interns receive accident insurance for the duration of their internship. Group medical insurance applies only to full-time employees.

## Parental leave
Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

## Wellness
Employees can claim a wellness allowance of up to 12,000 INR per year for gym, sports or wellbeing activities through the expense tool.

## Employee Assistance Programme
The confidential Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) offers free counselling for you and your family, 24/7. Contact details are in the HR Portal under "Wellbeing". Your use of EAP is never shared with your manager.

## Retirement
Full-time employees in India are enrolled in the Provident Fund as required by law. Contribution details appear on your payslip.
