## Our standard
Every Waters employee, intern and contractor is expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues with respect and follow the law and company policies. Managers are expected to model this standard and act on concerns raised with them.

## Dress code
Waters follows a business-casual dress code in offices. Client-facing meetings and lab areas may require specific attire or protective equipment; follow the site rules posted for that area.

## Social media
You may say you work at Waters, but do not share confidential information, customer names, unreleased products or internal screenshots. Only Corporate Communications speaks for the company publicly.

## Speaking up
If you see something that may break this code or the law, raise it with your manager, HR Business Partner, or the confidential Ethics Line. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.

## Consequences
Breaches of this code are reviewed by HR Policy & Ethics and may lead to corrective action up to and including termination, depending on severity.

## Annual acknowledgement
All employees complete the Code of Conduct acknowledgement in the Learning Portal within their first 30 days and every year after.
