## Leave types
Every full-time employee receives 18 days of earned (annual) leave, 10 days of sick leave and 2 floating holidays per calendar year. Interns receive 1 day of earned leave per completed month of internship plus sick leave as needed with manager approval. Leave entitlement is pro-rated in your joining year.

## How to apply for leave
Submit every leave request through the HR Portal under "Time Off". Planned leave should be requested at least 5 working days in advance. Your manager on record is the approver; HR Operations does not approve individual requests.

## Sick leave
Sick leave can be logged on the same day. For more than 2 consecutive sick days, upload a medical certificate in the HR Portal within 3 working days of returning.

## Carry forward and encashment
Up to 10 unused earned-leave days carry forward to the next year. Floating holidays and sick leave do not carry forward. Encashment applies only at exit, per local regulations.

## Holiday calendar
The approved public holiday calendar for each office is published in the HR Portal under "Holiday Calendar" each December for the following year.

## Leave balance
Your live leave balance is shown only in the HR Portal. IRA does not display or estimate individual balances.

## Attendance
Core collaboration hours are 10:30 AM to 4:30 PM local time. Repeated unplanned absence without notice is reviewed by your manager with HR Operations.
