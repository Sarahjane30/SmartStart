## Working hours
The standard working week is 40 hours. Core collaboration hours are 10:30 AM to 4:30 PM local time; outside those hours you may plan your day with your manager.

## Your first day
On Day 1 please arrive at the office reception by 9:30 AM. Your welcome and orientation session starts at 10:30 AM. Bring a government photo ID for your access badge.

## Hybrid work
Most roles follow a hybrid pattern of 3 office days per week. Interns are expected in the office 4 days per week during their first month so they can learn alongside their team. Your manager confirms your team's office days.

## Working from home
Work from home must be done from a private, secure location on company-managed equipment over VPN. Public Wi-Fi requires VPN at all times.

## Working from another country
Working from another country, even temporarily, needs written approval from your manager and HR Operations in advance because of tax, immigration and data rules.

## Office facilities and cafeteria
The cafeteria is on the ground floor and is open 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Meeting rooms are booked through Microsoft Outlook. Visitor access is arranged through reception at least one day ahead.

## Overtime
Non-exempt employees must get manager approval before working overtime. Overtime is recorded in the HR Portal timesheet.
