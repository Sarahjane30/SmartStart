## Classification levels
Waters data has four levels. Public: approved for release. Internal: default for everyday work. Confidential: customer data, source code, financials. Restricted: highly sensitive data such as health records, credentials and M&A plans.

## Where to store files
Store work files in OneDrive, SharePoint or Teams. Don't keep the only copy on your laptop, and never use personal cloud storage.

## Sharing externally
Share Internal or Confidential files externally only with people who need them, using SharePoint links with expiry. Restricted data needs data-owner approval and encryption.

## Email
Apply the matching sensitivity label in Outlook. Confidential and Restricted emails sent outside Waters are encrypted automatically.

## Printing and disposal
Collect printouts immediately and put confidential paper in the locked shred bins.
