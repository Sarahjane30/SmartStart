## Reporting phishing email
If an email looks suspicious or like phishing, don't click links or open attachments. Report it with the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook, which sends it to the SOC and removes it from your inbox.

## If you clicked a link
If you clicked a link or entered your password on a suspicious page, report it immediately to the IT Service Desk and change your password through Okta. Quick reporting prevents damage; you won't be penalised for reporting.

## Lost or stolen device
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT Service Desk within 1 hour so it can be locked and wiped. Also file a police report for theft.

## Other incidents
Report malware warnings, unexpected account activity, or data sent to the wrong person through the IT Service Desk as a Security Incident. The SOC monitors 24/7.

## Phishing simulations
Information Security runs regular phishing simulations. If you click one, you'll be assigned a short refresher module in the Learning Portal.
