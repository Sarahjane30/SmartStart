## Password rules
Passwords must be at least 14 characters. A passphrase of four or more unrelated words is recommended. Never reuse your Waters password on other sites and never share it, even with IT.

## Multi-factor authentication
MFA through the Okta Verify app is required for all Waters accounts. Set it up on Day 1 during IT orientation.

## Resetting your password
Reset your password yourself through the Okta self-service portal using MFA. If you are locked out or lose your MFA device, contact the IT Service Desk, which will verify your identity before resetting access.

## Password changes
Passwords don't expire on a schedule, but you must change yours immediately if you think it has been exposed.

## Password managers
Store work credentials only in the approved company password manager.
