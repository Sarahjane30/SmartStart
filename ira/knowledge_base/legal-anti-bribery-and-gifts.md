## No bribery
Never offer, give, ask for or accept anything of value to improperly influence a business decision. This applies to government officials and private companies alike, and to anyone acting on your behalf.

## Gifts
You may accept or give occasional gifts worth up to 5,000 INR (or local equivalent) if they are not cash and not linked to a pending decision. Anything above that must be declared in the Gifts Register and approved by your manager.

## Hospitality
Business meals and events are allowed when they are modest, infrequent and have a clear business purpose. Hospitality for government officials always needs prior Legal & Compliance approval.

## Conflicts of interest
Declare any situation where your personal interests could affect your work, such as a relative at a vendor or a second job, through the Conflict of Interest form in the HR Portal.

## Reporting
Report suspected bribery to Legal & Compliance or the Ethics Line. Reports can be anonymous.
