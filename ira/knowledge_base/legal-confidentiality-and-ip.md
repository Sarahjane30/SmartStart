## Confidential information
Anything not publicly released by Waters is confidential: customer data, product plans, source code, pricing and internal documents. Share it only with people who need it for their work, and never outside Waters without an approved NDA.

## NDA
You signed a confidentiality agreement as part of your offer. It continues to apply after you leave Waters.

## Who owns your work
Work you create as part of your job, including code, documents, designs and inventions, belongs to Waters. Report possible inventions to Legal & Compliance through the Invention Disclosure form.

## Open source
You may use open-source components that are on the approved license list. Contributing Waters code to public repositories needs approval from your manager and Legal & Compliance first.

## Side projects
Personal projects are allowed if they don't use Waters time, equipment or confidential information and don't compete with Waters. Declare them through the Conflict of Interest form if in doubt.

## Contracts
Only authorised signatories may sign contracts on behalf of Waters. Send any contract or NDA request to Legal & Compliance.
