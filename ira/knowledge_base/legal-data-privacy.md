## Personal data
Personal data is any information about an identifiable person, such as names, emails, phone numbers, employee IDs or health information. Waters processes personal data lawfully and only for a clear business purpose, in line with the DPDP Act (India) and GDPR where applicable.

## Your responsibilities
Only access personal data you need for your job. Never copy it to personal devices, personal email or unapproved tools, including public AI chatbots.

## Your own data
Waters uses your employee data to run payroll, benefits, IT access and onboarding. You can view and correct most of your personal details in the HR Portal and request other changes through HR Operations.

## Retention
Personal data is kept only as long as the retention schedule allows and then deleted or anonymised.

## Privacy incidents
If personal data is lost, sent to the wrong person or accessed without permission, report it to the Privacy Office and IT Service Desk immediately, within 24 hours at the latest.
